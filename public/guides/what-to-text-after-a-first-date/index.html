After the first date: what to text and when

Updated 2026-11-05

Short answer: If you liked her, text the same evening or the next morning. Say you had fun, mention one specific moment, and hint at a next time. No waiting games.

Texts that work

  • "Had a really good time tonight. Still laughing about the waiter and the candle"
  • "Thanks for tonight! I owe you a rematch at pool"
  • "That was fun. Let's do it again, my pick next time"
  • "Got home okay? I had a great time"

What to avoid

  • Waiting three days to "seem chill". Clear and honest beats strategic.
  • An essay about your feelings.
  • Asking "so what are we?" after one date.

If it didn't click

A kind, short message is enough: "Thanks for tonight, it was nice meeting you. I didn't feel a spark, but I wish you the best."

FAQ

Who should text first? Whoever wants to. If you enjoyed it, it's you. What if she doesn't reply? Give it a day; one follow-up at most.
