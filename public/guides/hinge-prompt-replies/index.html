Why prompts beat photos

A prompt comment shows you read her profile. Liking a photo with no comment is the easiest thing to ignore.

FAQ

Should I comment on a photo instead? Only with something specific in it (a place, a dog, an activity), never just looks. How long should a comment be? One sentence. What if she doesn't reply? Hinge chats die fast. Don't take it personally.
