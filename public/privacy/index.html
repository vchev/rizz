Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026

The short version: Coax has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your screenshots are read on your phone and never uploaded. To write replies, the text of the chat goes to our server and our AI provider, and neither of them keeps it.

What stays on your phone

  • Your screenshots. Coax reads the text in them on your iPhone (Apple's on-device text recognition). The images never leave your phone.
  • The chats Coax has read, your texting style (quiz answers and what Coax learned from your own messages), your settings, and simple counts such as how many replies you picked. These are stored in the app on your phone and included in your iPhone backup. Deleting the app deletes them.

What leaves your phone, and why

When you ask for replies, Coax sends: the text of the chat (up to the last 60 messages, marked as yours or theirs), which app it came from, the mood you picked, anything you typed into "Tell Coax what to say", your texting-style settings with a few of your own recent texts as examples, and whether you are under 18 (so replies stay age-appropriate).

This goes over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to our AI provider, Anthropic (the Claude API), to write the replies. The replies come back to your phone. Our server does not write the chat to disk or to logs. Anthropic processes it under a zero data retention agreement: it is not stored after the reply is written and is never used to train AI models.

Our host (Vercel) keeps standard technical logs, such as IP address and time of a request, for a short period to run and secure the service. These logs never contain your chats.

What we don't do

  • No account, name, email or phone number is needed.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking and no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs in the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Coax asks Apple only whether your subscription is active.

The free reply tool on this website

What you paste is sent to our AI provider to write one reply and is not stored. To limit free use, we keep a one-way hash of your IP address in memory for the day; it is never written to disk.

Ages

Coax is for people 16 and older. Users aged 16 and 17 get a stricter mode: no flirting about bodies, nothing sexual, no alcohol or drugs, and no plans to meet somewhere private.

Other people's messages

Chats include messages from the people you talk to. Coax uses them only to write your reply, keeps them only on your phone, and sends them only for that request.

Your choices

Turn off texting-style learning or reset it in the app's Settings. Delete the app to remove everything Coax stored. Because we don't keep your data on our servers, there is nothing else for us to delete; if you have a question or request, email press@hicoax.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
