**Short answer:** Ask fewer questions and share more. Give her short, specific things to react to, and move to a call or a date sooner. Some people just text dry and are great in person.

## Signs you're dealing with a dry texter (not a disinterested one)
- She replies, just briefly.
- She sometimes asks a question back.
- She says yes to plans.
If all three are missing, it may be low interest. See [one-word answers](/guides/what-to-text-one-word-answers/).

## What to send
- **A statement, not an interview:** "I just tried oat milk in my coffee and I've made a terrible mistake"
- **A this-or-that:** "Beach trip or cabin in the woods?"
- **A photo with one line:** a picture of your terrible cooking with "Rate it out of 10"
- **A plan:** "I'm better in person than over text. Drinks Thursday?"

## What not to do
- Fire off five questions in a row.
- Match her dryness. Two dry texters is a dead chat.
- Ask "why are you so dry?"

## FAQ
**How long should my texts be?** About the length of hers, plus one hook.
**Should I just ask her out?** Often yes. Dry texters tend to prefer meeting to typing.
