**Short answer:** Reply "hey" plus one specific thing: a callback to her profile, something you're doing, or an easy question. Never "hey" alone, and never three paragraphs.

## What "hey" usually means
"Hey" is low effort, not low interest. On dating apps it often means *I liked your profile but couldn't think of a line*. Your job is to make the second message easy.

## Replies that work
- **Use her profile:** "Hey! Okay, I need to know more about the dog in your third photo"
- **Use a prompt she answered:** "Hey :) Your 'worst travel story' answer can't just end there"
- **Use your day:** "Hey! Just survived a 3-hour meeting, so you're the best thing that's happened today"
- **Give an easy choice:** "Hey! Quick, coffee or cocktails person?"
- **Be honest and light:** "Hey! I was hoping you'd message, I'd been staring at your profile trying to be clever"

## What to avoid
- "Hey" back, alone. You've both now said nothing.
- Asking "how are you?" with nothing else. It leads to "good u?" loops.
- A compliment about looks only. Specific beats flattering.

## FAQ
**Should I wait before replying?** No. Reply when you see it; games are out ([clear communication is a top 2026 trend](https://www.freepressjournal.in/lifestyle/4-dating-trends-that-will-dominate-2026-gen-z-is-choosing-romance-that-is-all-about-clarity-clear-communication)).
**What if her profile is empty?** Use your own day or a choice question.
**What if she says "hey" again later?** Treat it as a restart; reference what you last talked about.
