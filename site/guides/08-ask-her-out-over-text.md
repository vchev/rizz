**Short answer:** Be specific: a place, a day, and a reason tied to your chat. "We should hang out sometime" is a maybe; "Tacos Thursday? You owe me a ranking" is a plan.

## When to ask
After a few good back-and-forths, usually within a few days of matching. Waiting weeks lets the chat go stale.

## 10 ways to ask
1. "You've talked about that ramen place twice now. Thursday?"
2. "I'm better in person than over text. Drinks this week?"
3. "Settle the pizza debate in person? Saturday at Joe's"
4. "There's a trivia night Wednesday. I need a teammate who knows things"
5. "Want to grab coffee Sunday morning? Low-key, no pressure"
6. "I'd like to keep this conversation going over a drink. Free Friday?"
7. "Going to the farmers market Saturday. Come judge my vegetable choices"
8. "You pick the place, I'll pick the day. Deal?"
9. "Let's test your claim that you're good at mini golf"
10. "I've enjoyed talking to you. Can I take you out this week?"

## If she says no or "maybe"
"No worries!" and move on, or offer one other day if she suggested she's busy. Don't push.

## FAQ
**Call or text to ask?** Text is normal now. A short call before meeting is a nice bonus.
**Should I suggest the place?** Yes. Making it easy is the point.
