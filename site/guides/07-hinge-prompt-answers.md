**Short answer:** Comment on the prompt, not her looks, and add a small twist: a playful disagreement, a follow-up, or a tiny plan. One line is plenty.

## 8 common prompts and replies that start a real conversation

| Her prompt | A reply that works |
| --- | --- |
| "My simple pleasures" (e.g. "a good coffee") | "Coffee order? I'm judging, kindly" |
| "I'm looking for" | "Someone who laughs at their own jokes is also on my list" |
| "Together, we could" (e.g. "find the best tacos") | "I accept this mission. Corn or flour?" |
| "A shower thought I recently had" | "Okay, now I can't stop thinking about it either. Thanks" |
| "The way to win me over is" | "Noted. What's the fastest way to lose you, so I avoid it?" |
| "Typical Sunday" | "This sounds great except the 7am part. Tell me you're joking" |
| "Unusual skills" | "I need proof. Video evidence, please" |
| "My most irrational fear" | "Okay, I need the story behind this" |

## Why prompts beat photos
A prompt comment shows you read her profile. Liking a photo with no comment is the easiest thing to ignore.

## FAQ
**Should I comment on a photo instead?** Only with something specific in it (a place, a dog, an activity), never just looks.
**How long should a comment be?** One sentence.
**What if she doesn't reply?** Hinge chats die fast. Don't take it personally.
