**Short answer:** Don't apologise or analyse. Open with something new and specific to her: a callback to an old joke, something that reminded you of her, or a simple plan.

## The 3 restart moves
1. **The callback:** "I finally watched the movie you said I'd hate. You were right"
2. **The reminder:** "Saw a golden retriever in a raincoat and thought of your dog"
3. **The plan:** "Hey stranger, a new bar opened near you. Want to try it this week?"

## Why it works
A specific detail shows you were actually paying attention, which is the opposite of the copy-paste openers people complain about. In a 2026 survey, 58% of singles said AI-written chats feel like "digital catfishing" ([Arrows](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/arrows-survey-ai-sees-widespread-use-and-dislike-in-dating/)). A real callback can't be faked.

## Timing
After a week or more of silence, one restart message is fair. After a month, open with a light acknowledgement: "Long time! How've you been?"

## FAQ
**What if I can't remember what we talked about?** Scroll up. Use the last real detail she shared.
**Should I mention why it died?** No. Just restart.
**What if she doesn't reply?** Then it's done, and that's fine.
